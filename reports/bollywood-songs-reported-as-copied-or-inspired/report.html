100 Bollywood songs carry corroborated claims of borrowed tunes — Pehli Nazar leads with 34 independent domains

Asked:

“Tell me which Bollywood songs are copied from other songs”

Two live-web searches over roughly 3,525 unique pages surfaced thousands of reported song pairings; after title normalization and a threshold of at least 3 independent domains, 100 Bollywood-song / earlier-song pairs remain, spanning films from the 1950s to the 2010s. These are documented claims — sources say “copied”, “lifted”, “inspired” or “tribute” — not legal rulings.

Findings the picture cannot say alone

Composer attribution is thin: 72 of the 100 pairs did not yield a reliable Hindi composer in extraction; among resolved names, Anu Malik, Pritam and Rajesh Roshan lead with 5 pairs each, and Pritam's pairs average 19.2 corroborating domains — itwofs.com
Three songs carry multiple claimed antecedents: Mehbooba Mehbooba (Say You Love Me and Ta Rialia), Jab Koi Baat Bigad Jaye (three variants of the Five Hundred Miles folk tune) and Mere Rang Mein Rangne Wali (The Final Countdown, twice normalized) — scroll.in
The same earlier song feeds two hits: Mory Kanté's Tama is reported behind both Tamma Tamma (20 domains) and Jumma Chumma (19), and the composers fought over the credit — indianexpress.com
Only 25 of 100 rows carry an explicit relationship word from sources ("copied", "lifted", "tribute", "recycling"); the rest are reported pairings without a stated verb — huffpost.com
