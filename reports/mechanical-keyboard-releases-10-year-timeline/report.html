592 mechanical keyboard releases in ten years — the web record surges in 2025–26

Asked:

“show me a comprehensive timeline of release dates of mechanical keyboards over the last 10 years. go month by month with date filters etc.”

A web-sourced index of 592 distinct mechanical-keyboard model releases across the 120 months from September 2016 through August 2026, assembled from manufacturer announcements, press releases and specialist publications. 369 entries carry an exact day; 223 are dated to the month only. 2016 and 2026 are partial years, and a zero month means no qualifying release was located in the scanned sources — not proof none existed.

Findings

January 2026 is the busiest month in the record with 26 releases, part of a 2025–26 surge that may partly reflect better current-web discoverability rather than pure market growth. pmarketresearch.com
Only 7 of 120 months yielded zero located releases — July 2017, January–February 2018, June 2020, July 2021, June 2023 and May 2024. mechanical-keyboard.org
2025 alone contributes 94 models from 67 source pages, roughly double most earlier full years, led by launches such as the Keychron Q Ultra ZMK. finance.yahoo.com
581 of 592 entries were dated on a generic release/launch/availability basis; only 11 sources named a specific basis such as shipping, introduction or general availability. daskeyboard.com

Method: web-sourced index of mechanical-keyboard releases, 592 model rows across 120 months (2016-09 to 2026-08), built from two broad web searches over ~4,500 URL-deduplicated pages with semantic extraction capped at the first 2,000 results per pass — an extensive index, not a census. Switches, keycaps, accessories, musical keyboards, colour-only variants and later reviews were excluded; names were normalized while generations, sizes and layouts stayed separate; conflicts resolved toward the earliest, most precise, most primary date. 369 rows are day-precise, 223 month-only. Zero months mean nothing was located in scanned sources.
