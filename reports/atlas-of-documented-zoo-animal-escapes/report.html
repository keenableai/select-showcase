500 documented escapes, mapped where the record allows: exact mileage is rare, so "how far" is most often the farthest named place

Asked:

“Map every zoo animal escape: which animal, how far it got, how it was caught”

“Every” is not literally knowable. This is a live-web census: 12 searches over 1,847 unique pages yielded 2,502 extracted incident mentions, consolidated here into 500 animal-level records spanning 1916–2024. 263 of the 500 could be placed on the map from facility or city names; the rest keep their story but not a pin. Distances, outcomes and durations follow the source wording — blanks stay blank.

What the record says the visual cannot

Recapture or return is the modal ending — 297 of 500 records — while 89 animals were shot or euthanised and 38 were never found, including Sedgwick County's Flamingo No. 492, later seen on the Texas coast. mygreenworld.org
Only 173 of 500 records state any reach at all, and most give a place, not a distance — a tree in a nearby botanical park, a highway interchange, the Adams Morgan neighborhood. livescience.com
The single worst event is Zanesville, Ohio, 2011: 56 animals released from the Muskingum County Animal Farm, of which 48 were killed, six captured, and two presumed eaten. en.wikipedia.org
Big cats (115 records) and primates (104) dominate the documented set — a coverage bias as much as an escape pattern, since a loose tiger makes news where a loose peafowl may not. en.wikipedia.org

Method: a live-web census across 12 searches and 1,847 unique result pages produced 2,502 extracted incident mentions from 1,228 source pages (2,335 named a facility, 1,008 stated a distance or farthest place, 2,360 an outcome, 39 carried a machine-extracted city or country), spanning 1916–2024. Duplicate news coverage was normalized by animal label into the 500 records shown; extraction may merge variants of one incident, and historical or unreported escapes are necessarily missing — this is not a definitive universe of all escapes. Map pins are placed at named facilities via a hand-built gazetteer; 237 records without a resolvable place are listed but not mapped. Distance and outcome wording is the source's own.
