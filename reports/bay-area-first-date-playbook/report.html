The playbook

Before and during

  1. Propose a specific place, day, and time — decisiveness is kind, and it gives the other person something concrete to say yes to.
  2. Meet there rather than arranging a pickup; a public spot keeps things comfortable for both of you.
  3. Confirm on the day, and arrive on time.
  4. Ask open questions and share about yourself in return — a conversation, not an interview.
  5. Keep your phone away and face down.
  6. Offer to split; whatever you agree, handle payment graciously and without ceremony.
  7. Respect boundaries, and end clearly: an honest thank-you, or a specific second-date invitation.

Bay Area logistics

  • Pick a spot near BART or Caltrain, or one that's fair to both commutes — nobody should cross two bridges for coffee.
  • Allow real time for traffic and parking; arriving flustered is a worse start than arriving early.
  • Bring a layer. Microclimates mean the Warming Hut and downtown San José can be twenty degrees apart.
  • Check official hours and prices before leaving — the costs and times shown here are as stated by each source and may have changed.
  • Keep the plan to one simple activity with an optional extension nearby; escalate to booked, paid experiences only once interest is mutual.
