What the tree cannot say in one glance

Gordon Ramsay is the network’s biggest sponge: he trained under five masters — Albert Roux, Marco Pierre White, Pierre Koffmann, Guy Savoy, and Joël Robuchon — before minting five protégés of his own. grokipedia.com
Fernand Point’s La Pyramide seeded French haute cuisine: Paul Bocuse, Alain Chapel, both Troisgros brothers, and Louis Outhier all passed through his kitchen — and Bocuse also apprenticed with Eugénie Brazier. nytimes.com
Ferran Adrià’s elBulli is the single widest teaching kitchen in the set, with eight alumni including René Redzepi, Massimo Bottura, José Andrés, Joan Roca, and Grant Achatz. britannica.com
The bridges make it a network, not a pedigree: René Redzepi worked at both elBulli and The French Laundry, and Grant Achatz passed through Charlie Trotter’s, The French Laundry, and elBulli. guide.michelin.com
