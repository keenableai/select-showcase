Nuzvid in Andhra Pradesh alone is the recorded origin of three rasalu-type varieties — Cherukurasamu, Chinna rasalu and Peddarasamu. en.wikipedia.org
West Bengal's six varieties concentrate in just two districts: Malda (Khirsapati, Lakshmanbhog, Fazli) and Murshidabad (Himsagar, Kishan Bhog, Kohitoor). indianeconomicobserver.com
Uttar Pradesh holds four historically prominent origins — Dashehari of Malihabad, Langda of Varanasi, Chaunsa of Hardoi and Anwar Ratol of Rataul — with the strongest sourcing in the set (28 mentions). agriculturewale.com
Amrapali is the only lab-bred entry: a 1970s hybrid whose birthplace is IARI, New Delhi rather than an orchard district. svpss.in
